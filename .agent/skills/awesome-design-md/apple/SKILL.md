# Apple Inspired Design System

Use this skill for premium consumer products and applications that aim for a high-end, aspirational, and "magic" feel.

## Core Vibe
- Radical simplicity and white space
- SF Pro typography
- Cinematic product imagery
- Pill-shaped components and huge corner radiuses

## Design Tokens

### Colors
- **Monochrome**: Pure White, Pure Black, and System Gray.
- **Accent**: #0071e3 (Apple Blue)
- **Blur**: Frosted glass effects (Backdrop Filter)

### Typography
- **Font**: SF Pro (or high-quality Sans-serif)
- **Scale**: Hero headers are massive and bold.

### Components
- **Buttons**: Pill-shaped, blue primary.
- **Cards**: Large corner radiuses (20px+), diffuse shadows.

## Prompts for Implementation
- "Build a product showcase page with Apple's aesthetic: massive white space, bold SF Pro type, and glass effects."
- "Apply Apple's pill-shaped buttons and large corner radiuses to this mobile app mockup."
